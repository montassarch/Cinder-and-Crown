# Enemy art

Rendered in Blender (`tools/art/enemy_render.py`) and shrunk to pixel art (`tools/art/enemy_pixel.py`).

redistributed as assets. Only the finished frames are kept here; download the packs again to regenerate.

| Enemy | Built from |
|---|---|
| Shambler | Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy (Male Peasant), undead tint, zombie head added |
| Crown Guard | Male Peasant + helm, breastplate, pauldrons and sword made in Blender |
| Penitent | Male Ranger (hooded), pale tint, tower shield and spear made in Blender |
| Powder Pilgrim | Bestiary - Dungeon Monsters Kit (Puglin) + a powder keg made in Blender |
| Crown Brute | Bestiary - Dungeon Monsters Kit (Imp) + a crown made in Blender |
| Ember Wisp, Cinder Bat | Made from scratch in Blender |

Animations come from Quaternius's **Universal Animation Library** (Idle, Walk, Jog, Sprint, Sword Attack,
Punch Jab, Spell, Hit, Death).
