# Black swordsman art

Derived from **Hero Knight** by Sven Thole — https://sventhole.itch.io/hero-knight
(100 hand-drawn frames). The pack's license allows editing and adapting it for any game
project, commercial or not; the raw assets may NOT be redistributed or resold on their own.
Keep this repository private, or remove `tools/art/source/` and `art/black_swordsman/` before
making it public.

Changes made here (see `tools/art/black_swordsman.py`): the blue-steel armor is recolored to near-black
plate with bright edge highlights, and a second copy of every frame has a black plague-style
mask with a glowing red eye baked in. Run the tool again to regenerate both from the originals.
