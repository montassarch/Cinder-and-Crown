# Playtest report, 2026-11-03 (playtester agent)

Builds: 26a26f9 and f32dae1 (feature/juice). 35 bot runs (~5 h game time, 4,700+ kills, ~130 boss fights, starts at
depths 0-11, five with a fresh save), ~230 real-engine screenshots at 844x390 (iPhone landscape), the web build and the
live /latest/ build driven in Playwright with touch. Evidence files were kept in the session scratchpad (qa/).

No critical issue: no crashes, no game script errors, node count flat (216-341), no orphans, nothing fell out of the world.

## High
- **H1. Beating the Storm King does not end the run.** After VICTORY the exit leads to endless Storm Spire levels
  (depth 12, 13...). Nothing listens to `GeneratedLevel.run_won`. Fix: an end-of-run screen, record the win, new run.
- **H2. The right-hand button cluster hides about the right third of the playfield** (at 844x390 the view is ~467 base
  px wide, the cluster covers the right ~166). Bosses, wisps (which keep 7-8 tiles away) and wind-up rings sit under the
  buttons. Fix: lighter button fills, a camera lead so the hero sits left of centre, tells drawn above the controls.
- **H3. The middle ledge of every boss arena is a complete safe spot.** A boss under it never starts a move (the chaser
  branch only attacks when the hero is within 2 tiles vertically). Fix: bosses skip that check; hazards already aim at
  the ledge.
- **H4. No invulnerability after a hit**, so hits stack (two knights 0.01 s apart, two bolts in one frame); burst
  deaths within seconds of entering a level. Fix: ~0.5 s of post-hit invulnerability with a blink; stagger identical
  enemies' first attacks.
- **H5. Difficulty is lopsided.** Hollow Bishop trivial (median 21 s), Storm King a wall (1 win in 3 natural arrivals,
  0 in 12 fresh tries). Fix: Bishop ~750 HP; slow the wave budget from depth 9; Storm King phase 2 summons fewer wisps or
  weaker bolts; re-tune after H4.

## Medium
- **M1.** Standing in the exit when the last enemy dies never opens the boon picker (only offered on entering).
- **M2.** The hero is drawn behind every enemy and disappears inside bosses (enemies are added after him).
- **M3.** World and victory banners run under the buttons.
- **M4.** HUD labels have no outline: white on white over the Frost Ossuary ice.
- **M5.** Waves still stack past six enemies (8-9 alive at once).
- **M6.** Parry is useless against the Rime Colossus and the Forge Tyrant (no parryable moves).
- **M7.** Hazard eruptions read well now, but the warning before them (a 3 px floor ellipse) is faint on a phone.

## Low
- L1 end walls are plain floor tiles filling 35-40% of the screen at the level ends; L2 Esc can unpause behind the boon
  picker or shop; L3 the flask at full health gives no feedback; L4 sandbox menu buttons are small for touch; L5 the exit
  gate is a flat rectangle; L6 boons and the shop say "sword" but apply to every weapon; L7 meta progression is short
  (all upgrades cost 174 cells, one Storm King pays 120); L8 the phone opens into the sandbox (title screen in Stage 5);
  L9 first download ~17 MB (wasm 10.1 MB gzipped + pck 7.1 MB), engine-ready in 5-9 s on a fast connection.

## Verified working
Suite 645/645 on f32dae1. All four bosses appear, show their bar, change phase at half health and open the exit; every
move fires; summons respect the cap and crumble. Dying in a boss fight goes to the shop and back to depth 0 cleanly.
Swapping weapons mid-fight, the World picker and all spawn buttons (bosses included) work. On the phone: boots with no
console errors, Storm/Rend/Swap work, the stick works together with attack/jump/rend, boon cards fit. The worlds read as
distinct and moody; fire, ice and lightning hazards look good.

Bot notes: status lines repeat during hit-stop; "hero stuck" can fire right after a level change; the bot shares the
real save (already maxed), so its balance numbers are for a maxed hero.
