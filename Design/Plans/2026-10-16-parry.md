# Parry and riposte

Branch: `feature/boons` (same line as the boons). Test build: `/parry-1/` on the game site.

## How it plays
- **PARRY** button (touch: just left of JMP; keyboard: L; pad: left-stick click). It plays a 0.5s guard.
- A strike that lands in the first **0.25s** is deflected: no damage, no hurt stagger, no plague mask.
- The first **0.1s** is a **perfect** parry: longer enemy stagger, bigger riposte, gold spark.
- The deflected enemy is staggered (0.8s, 1.3s perfect) and shoved back.
- **Riposte:** the next sword hit within 1.2s deals x2 damage (x3 after a perfect parry). It is used up by the first hit.
- After a successful parry, attack or roll cuts the recovery short. A whiffed parry is committed, then a 0.3s cooldown stops button mashing.

## How it is built
- `PlayerStateMachine`: new `PARRY` state; ctx keys `parry_buffered`, `parry_ready`, `parry_duration`, `parry_succeeded`.
- `Player.take_strike(damage, source_x, attacker)` is the parryable path. Enemies call it when the victim has it, otherwise `take_hit` (so hazards and stand-in test victims are unaffected). `take_hit` itself cannot be parried.
- `Enemy.on_parried(perfect, from_x)` staggers via the existing stun and applies knockback. A strike that was parried no longer falls through into recovery.
- `Weapon.next_hit_multiplier` carries the riposte; the player clears it when the hit lands, the window ends, or a new run starts.
- `ParryEffect` draws the spark ring in real time so it shows through the hit-stop.
- Numbers live as constants at the top of `player.gd` (`PARRY_*`, `RIPOSTE_*`) and `enemy.gd` (`PARRY_STAGGER`).
