# Touch UI: Wild Rift style

Branch: `feature/touch-ui` (on top of `feature/skeleton-knight`). Status: **built**, test build `/touch-1/`.

## What it is
- **Floating stick (left):** a ring and knob that appears under the left thumb (anywhere in the left ~40% of the screen, below the HUD), kept fully on screen; a faint ring shows where it rests.
- **Thumb cluster (right):** a big Attack button in the corner, with the other seven fanned round it on two arcs. Inner arc: Roll, Parry, Jump. Outer arc: Storm, Rend, Flask, Swap weapon.
- **Vector icons** (no words): sword, roll arrow, shield, chevrons, blue bolt, red lightning, flask, swap arrows. Drawn from shapes, so they stay crisp at any screen size.
- **Feedback:** a pressed button shrinks and brightens; Storm and Rend dim with a recharge sweep while the mana is short; the flask dims when empty and wears a charge badge.
- The overlay redraws only when something changed (not every frame).
- Buttons are fixed on the arc (dragging them into place was offered and not chosen).

## Where
`game/src/input/touch_controls.gd`, bound to the player in `levels/sandbox.gd`; tests in `tests/test_touch_controls.gd`.
