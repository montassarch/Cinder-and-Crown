# Enemy telegraph, poise and guard break

Branch: `feature/boons`. Test build: `/poise-1/` on the game site. Second Graveflame port after boons and parry.

## What changed
- **Telegraph tell:** a winding-up enemy shows a ring and "!" over its head. The ring closes exactly as the strike lands (the parry cue); it turns yellow once the strike is committed.
- **Flinch:** hitting a light enemy (poise 0) while it walks or early in its wind-up interrupts it for 0.2s. Past half the wind-up it is **committed**: the hit still damages it but the strike goes ahead. Recovering or reeling enemies do not flinch again.
- **Poise:** `EnemyData.poise` (hits it shrugs off). The Crown Brute has 4; Shambler and Cinder Bat have 0. Poised enemies never flinch; each hit costs one poise, shown as pips above the head. Poise refills after 2s without a hit.
- **Guard break:** at 0 poise the enemy is staggered for 1.6s (even mid-strike, which cancels the strike), turns orange, and takes x1.5 damage until it recovers. Poise then resets to full.
- Parry and Storm stun still work on top of all this.

## Where
`src/enemies/enemy.gd` (constants `COMMIT_FRACTION`, `FLINCH_SECONDS`, `BREAK_*`, `POISE_REGEN_DELAY`, `TELL_*`), `enemy_data.gd`, `data/enemies/crown_brute.tres`. Tests: `tests/test_enemy_poise.gd`.
