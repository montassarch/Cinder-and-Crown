# The full game: plan

Asked for on 2026-10-29: "create the full game": different maps and backgrounds, different bosses, lots of magic VFX,
3D models and swords made in Blender, a lot of movement and combat. Test everything fully before starting the next stage.

Every stage ends the same way: the whole test suite green, the playtest bot run through the new content, real-engine
screenshots checked by eye, a test build on the fixed link (`/latest/`), notes here and in the backlog, commit and push.

## Stage 1: four worlds
A run crosses four worlds of three levels each (`data/biomes/*.tres`, `BiomeData`):

| Depths | World | Look | Weather | New enemies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2 | The Cinder Cathedral | stone, stained glass, braziers | embers | (the first seven) |
| 3-5 | The Frostbound Ossuary | ice crypt of skulls, blue crystals | snow | Frost Ghoul, Rime Wisp |
| 6-8 | The Molten Forge | iron plates, furnaces, lava falls | ash | Forge Brute, Magma Bat |
| 9-11 | The Storm Spire | castle top in a night storm, lanterns | rain + lightning flashes | Storm Knight, Storm Wisp |

- Art: `tools/art/biomes.py` paints each world's four parallax layers, fog, floor and ledge tiles, glow and light frames.
- New enemies: rendered in Blender by `tools/art/enemy_render.py` (Quaternius models + props made in Blender).
- Rooms wear the world's tiles; four new room layouts (staircase, twin towers, long gallery, broken bridge).
- A world's name is announced across the screen when the hero arrives. The menu's World picker jumps to a world.
- **Done 2026-10-30**, test build on `/latest/`. Checked: 620 tests green; the bot played 8 game minutes from the start
  through all four worlds (15 levels, 214 kills, no errors, node count flat, no leaks) plus 4-minute runs starting in
  each new world; real-engine screenshots of every world; the web build boots in a phone-sized browser with no errors.

## Stage 2: bosses (one per world, on its last level)
The last level of each world (depths 2, 5, 8, 11) is spawn room, boss arena, exit; the exit opens when the boss falls.
A boss is an enemy with a second phase (new moves, faster) below half health, a health bar across the top of the
screen, and a new move kind, HAZARD: marks spots on the floor (a warning glow), then the hand-painted effect plays there
and hurts the hero if he is still standing in it (fire pillars, falling ice swords, lightning, a floor that breaks).

| World | Boss | Built from | Phase 1 | Phase 2 adds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cathedral | The Hollow Bishop | Ranger outfit, 1.8x, mitre and crozier | crozier sweep, fire pillars, summons shamblers | floor waves, more pillars |
| Ossuary | The Rime Colossus | Imp, 2.3x, ice crystals | charge, falling ice swords | slam waves, bigger blade rain |
| Forge | The Forge Tyrant | Imp, 2.2x, giant hammer | hammer slam (floor breaks), charge | fire pillars, summons magma bats |
| Spire | The Storm King | hero base model, 1.6x, crown, storm sword | sword string, lightning strikes | lunges, summons storm wisps |

Beating the Storm King wins the run.
## Stage 3: movement, combat, 3D weapons
- Double jump (a burst under the feet) and an air dash (roll in the air), so fights move in the air too.
- The hero rendered in Blender holding each of the five weapons, each with its own attack animations from the
  library (sword swings, spear thrusts, bow aiming and shooting, wand casts, gauntlet punches).
- Weapons found in a run: a beaten boss offers a choice of two weapons (or keep yours).
