# Boons (first Graveflame port)

Branch: `feature/boons` (built on `spike/abilities-complete`). Test build: `/boons-1/` on the game site.

## What it does
Reach the exit of a generated level and the game pauses on a **pick-one-of-three** screen. The chosen boon lasts until the run ends (death or "Reset player" starts a new run).

- `BoonData` (`src/boons/boon_data.gd`): one `.tres` per boon in `game/data/boons/`. Drop a file in, it is offered. Fields: id (= file name), name, description, effect, amount, rarity, unique.
- Effects so far: max health, heal, damage %, speed %, lifesteal (HP per hit), mana gain %.
- `RunBuild` (`src/boons/run_build.gd`): pure state. Rarity weights 60/30/10, a repeat penalty (`1 / (1 + 1.5 * times shown)`), unique boons leave the pool once taken. Offers are seeded by the level seed, so a seed replays.
- `BoonPicker` (`src/ui/boon_picker.gd`): three thumb-sized cards; tap or press 1-3.
- 12 boons ship: 5 common, 4 rare, 3 epic (epics are unique).

## Credit
The offer roll and the boon-card idea are ported from Graveflame (bindusara-reddy/Graveflame, MIT). Names, numbers and code are our own.

## Bug found on the way
A level built this frame could report the hero "entering" its exit a frame later, after he had already been moved to the spawn. That chained straight into the next level. `GeneratedLevel._on_exit_entered` now checks that the hero's body really overlaps an exit zone.
