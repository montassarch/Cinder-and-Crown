# Five weapons, each with its own moves

Branch: `feature/juice`. Asked for on 2026-10-25: sword, spear, bow, magic wand, gauntlet; the sword has 3 attack patterns, the spear 2; every weapon has 3-4 moves, each with its own movement. The magic effects and animations are placeholders for now ("we discuss the magic later").

## What a weapon is now
A weapon (`WeaponData`) is a set of moves (`WeaponMove`, one `.tres` sub-resource each):
- a **tap chain**: tap again in time (0.4 s) for the next strike; it starts over after a pause or after an air or roll move;
- an **air move**: attack while jumping or falling;
- a **roll move**: attack within 0.25 s after a roll.

Each move sets its own damage multiplier, duration, hit window, hit box shape, knockback, hit stop, forward lunge (negative = hop back), dive speed (air moves), hero animation and slash arc. Projectile moves (bow, wand) fire shots instead: count, fan angle, aim angle, speed, range, pierce, colour.

| Weapon | Chain | Air | Roll |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rusty Blade (sword) | Slash, Rising Cut, Crushing Blow | Plunge (dives down) | Roll Cut (long lunge) |
| Ember Spear | Thrust, Skewer (long reach, lunges) | Dive Spear (tall hit box below) | Charge (biggest lunge) |
| Iron Gauntlet | Jab, Cross, Uppercut (fast, short) | Ground Slam (dives) | Shoulder Charge |
| Hunter's Bow | Shot, Power Shot (pierces 2) (both step back) | Falling Shot (aims down) | Volley (3 arrows) |
| Ash Wand (placeholder) | Bolt, Arc Orb (slow, pierces 3) | Meteor Spark (aims down) | Scatter (5 orbs) |

The spear reuses the sword's slash arc art with a different, longer and narrower hit box (as asked: same effect, different hitting).

## Where it lives
`src/weapons/` (`weapon_move.gd`, `weapon_data.gd`, `weapon.gd` runs one move at a time, `player_projectile.gd`), `data/weapons/*.tres`. The player picks the move in `Player._start_move`; the effects listen to `move_started`. In the sandbox menu, Slot A and Slot B can hold any of the five (swap with the swap button).

## Known gaps
- The hero art always holds a sword, and the bow, wand, spear and gauntlet reuse the same animation set (sword swings, the parry thrust, the storm raise). Real per-weapon animations and weapon models are a Blender job.
- Arrows are glowing darts, the wand's shots are plain orbs: placeholders.
- No weapon pickup or shop yet: runs still start with sword + spear; the other three are for the sandbox.
- Balance is first-guess.

## Magic effect ideas (concept pictures, not in the game)
Six effects for the wand, drawn as glowing Blender renders (`game/tools/art/magic_render.py`) and placed on a real game screenshot: Arcane Bolt (fast violet comet), Fire Orb (slow, explodes on a group), Frost Shards (fan of five ice spears, slows), Chain Lightning (jumps between up to three enemies, stuns), Void Orb (pulls enemies together), Rune Nova (rune circle with pillars of light under enemies). See `Design/Concepts/2026-10-25-magic-effects-in-game.png`. To build one for real: animated frames (the stills show one flight and one impact moment), a move in the wand's `.tres`, and a small code behaviour for the new ones (chaining, pulling, slowing, ground circle).

Frost Blade Fall (ice sword falls, crystals burst), Tidal Slash (water crescent), Arcane Ward (shield dome), Ember Blade (flame twists into a sword that flies), Ember Crash (floor breaks, fire bursts), Fire Pillar.
