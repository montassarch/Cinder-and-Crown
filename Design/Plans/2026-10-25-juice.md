# Juice pass: making combat feel good

Branch: `feature/juice` (on top of `feature/touch-ui`). Inspired by Shadow Hunter: Lost World and the usual hack-and-slash feel toolbox. The game felt bland: it needed effects, impact and flow.

## Slices
1. **Hit feel (built, test build `/juice-1/`):** a smooth glowing slash arc on every swing (a different arc for each of the three combo hits, blue while Storm is charged), a spark burst and a floating damage number where a hit lands, the enemy flashes white when hit, a ring and spark burst when an enemy dies, a ring when the hero parries (bigger and gold when perfect), and screen shake scaled to the hit (also when the hero is hurt).
2. **Movement feel:** dust puffs on run start, landing and roll; roll afterimage; jump and land squash.
3. **Abilities:** screen flash, camera punch and ground cracks for Storm and Rend.
4. **Atmosphere:** light shafts, fog, vignette, colour grade, a glow pass if the renderer allows it.
5. **UI juice:** a health bar with a delayed damage trail, button press pulses, combo counter.
6. **Animation:** more frames and custom combo poses (the library has one sword swing), anticipation and follow-through.

## How slice 1 is built
- Art: `tools/art/fx_render.py` (Blender) draws the slash arcs, spark and ring as glowing frames on black; they are added over the scene and tinted in code. Frames in `art/fx/`.
- Code: `src/fx/` (`FxArt`, `FxSprite`, `CameraShake`, `DamageNumber`, `CombatFx`). The player emits `swung` and `hit_landed`; `CombatFx` listens, and the level hands it every enemy it spawns. Enemies flash through a small shader (`hit_flash.gdshader`).
- Tests: `tests/test_fx.gd`, `tests/test_combat_fx.gd`.
