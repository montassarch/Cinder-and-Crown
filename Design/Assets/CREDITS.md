# Credits for the art library (`game/art/library`)

Copy the **Required** block into the game's credits screen for every asset from it that ships. The **Optional** list is
CC0 / public-domain work: no credit is needed, but naming the authors is kind and costs nothing.
Full details (source URL, licence, where the files are) are in [CATALOGUE.md](CATALOGUE.md).
